You've just joined a secret drone program. Before any unit goes into the field, its onboard AI has to prove itself here, on a bordered 10x10 calibration grid.

You're the engineer. Every command you queue up — where to land, which way to turn, when to move, when to strike — is a drill its AI has to learn. Run it, and you're training the model, one sequence at a time.

The grid enforces its own limits. The drone never crosses the edge, and it always reports back exactly where it's standing, so you can calibrate with confidence.

How it works

  • Place sets the drone down on the grid at an X, Y position, facing north, south, east or west. Nothing else works until it's placed.
  • Left and Right rotate the drone 90° on the spot — its position doesn't change.
  • Move steps the drone forward one square in the direction it's facing. It's blocked from stepping off the edge of the grid.
  • Attack fires two squares ahead in the direction it's facing. If there isn't clear space for both squares, the attack is skipped.
  • Report announces the drone's current X, Y position and the direction it's facing.
  • Build a list of commands, then hit Play to run them in order, one at a time.
