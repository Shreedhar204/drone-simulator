You've just joined a secret drone program. Before any unit goes into the field, its onboard AI has to prove itself here, on a bordered 10x10 calibration grid.

You're the engineer. Every command you queue up — where to land, which way to turn, when to move, when to strike — is a drill its AI has to learn. Run it, and you're training the model, one sequence at a time.

The grid enforces its own limits. The drone never crosses the edge, and it always reports back exactly where it's standing, so you can calibrate with confidence.

How it works

  • Place sets the drone down — pick X, Y and a facing direction.
  • Left / Right turns it. Move steps it forward one square.
  • Attack fires two squares ahead — it won't fire without room.
  • Report calls out the drone's current position.
  • Build a list of commands, then hit Play to watch them run, one at a time.
